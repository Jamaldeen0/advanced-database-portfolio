# Case Study: ZAARH Collections

ZAARH Collections is an early-stage perfume oil business in Nigeria. Concentrated oils are blended to create scents, which are sold as finished perfume oil products to both retail and wholesale customers.

The sole user of the database is the business owner. The database stores customers, products, orders and order items, payments, scent recipes, and raw ingredients. It supports recording customer orders, tracking payments (including part-payments), storing the formula for each scent, tracking product and ingredient stock, and distinguishing retail from wholesale customers.
